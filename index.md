## About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Machine Learning & Data Science at <a class="school-link" href="https://www.gsu.edu/">Georgia State University</a>, advised by <a href="https://chikuang.github.io/" style="font-weight:bold;">Dr. Chi-Kuang Yeh</a>. Before that, I received an M.S. in Statistics from <a class="school-link" href="https://msu.edu/">Michigan State University</a> and a B.S. in Financial Mathematics from the <a class="school-link" href="https://english.uibe.edu.cn/">University of International Business and Economics</a>.

## Research Interests

My research develops statistical methods for reliable, uncertainty-aware inference, with functional data analysis at its core and a growing focus on the trustworthy evaluation of large language models.

- **Functional Data Analysis:** functional time series, long-run covariance, bandwidth selection
- **Trustworthy LLM Evaluation:** LLM-as-a-judge auditing, uncertainty quantification
- **Statistical Machine Learning:** deep learning theory, graph neural networks
